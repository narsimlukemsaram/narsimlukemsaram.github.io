Bio

Dr. Narsimlu Kemsaram is a Research Fellow in Multi-Robot Systems with the Department of Computer Science at the University College London (UCL), London, United Kingdom. He holds a Bachelor's, Master's, and PhD in Computer Science. His PhD thesis is on the topic of "Design and Development of Hybrid Autonomous Visual Tracking Algorithm for Unmanned Aerial Vehicles."

He has held postdoctoral and research roles in Germany, the Netherlands, and Luxembourg, contributing to EU-H2020, EPSRC, and national research projects related to cooperative robotics, stereo perception, and acoustophoresis. He currently works with the Multi-Sensory Devices (MSD) Group at UCL on the EPSRC Prosperity Partnership project Swarm SSMs.
